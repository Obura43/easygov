Digital ID Services

Your complete guide to accessing Digital ID (e-ID) services in Kenya.

Overview

The Digital ID (e-ID) is Kenya’s electronic national identification system that enhances security and access to government services. You can apply and manage your digital ID through government portals and Huduma Centres.

How to Apply

  1. Step 1:

    Register or login to the official digital ID portal or visit a Huduma Centre.

  2. Step 2:

    Fill the application form with accurate personal details.

  3. Step 3:

    Upload or submit required documents for verification.

  4. Step 4:

    Make any applicable payments as required.

  5. Step 5:

    Track your application status online or via Huduma Centre notifications and collect your Digital ID when ready.

Required Documents

  • Valid National ID card (if renewing/upgrading)
  • Birth Certificate (for first-time applicants)
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • KRA PIN certificate (where applicable)
  • Any other documents specified by the digital ID application guidelines
