HELB Services

Your complete guide to accessing HELB (Higher Education Loans Board) services in Kenya.

Overview

The Higher Education Loans Board (HELB) provides student loans and bursaries to eligible Kenyan students. Services include loan applications, repayment, loan statements, and more through government digital platforms.

How to Apply

  1. Step 1:

    Register or log in to the HELB e-portal.

  2. Step 2:

    Complete the online application form with your academic and personal details.

  3. Step 3:

    Upload all required supporting documents as specified.

  4. Step 4:

    Submit your application before the deadline.

  5. Step 5:

    Track your application status via the HELB portal and receive notifications.

Required Documents

  • Valid National ID card or Birth Certificate
  • KRA PIN certificate
  • Admission letter or school registration number
  • Academic transcripts or certificates (if applicable)
  • Passport-sized photos
  • Any other supporting documents as required by HELB
