KRA Services Guide

Complete guide to Kenya Revenue Authority services and tax filing

KRA PIN Registration

  1. Visit iTax Portal

    Go to itax.kra.go.ke and click "Register PIN"

  2. Select Registration Type

    Choose individual, business, or non-resident option

  3. Fill Personal Details

    Enter ID number, phone, email, and personal information

  4. Submit Application

    Review details and submit. PIN is sent via SMS and email instantly

Filing Income Tax Returns

All Kenyans with a KRA PIN must file annual tax returns by June 30th each year.

  1. Login to iTax

    Access your account at itax.kra.go.ke

  2. Select Return Type

    Choose the appropriate return form based on your income source

  3. Declare Income

    Enter all sources of income including employment, business, rental, etc.

  4. Claim Reliefs

    Apply eligible tax reliefs and deductions

  5. Submit & Acknowledge

    Review, submit, and download acknowledgment receipt

Common KRA Services

  • PIN Registration & Verification
  • Income Tax Returns Filing
  • VAT Registration & Returns
  • Tax Compliance Certificates
  • Pay As You Earn (PAYE) Returns
  • Excise Duty Returns
  • Customs Services

Important Deadlines

  • Individual Tax Returns: June 30th annually
  • Corporate Tax Returns: 6 months after financial year end
  • VAT Returns: 20th of following month
  • PAYE Returns: 9th of following month
