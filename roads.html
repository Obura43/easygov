Roads & Transport Projects

Building Kenya's infrastructure for economic growth and connectivity

Home / National Projects / Roads & Transport

Overview

Kenya's road and transport infrastructure is critical to economic development, trade, and regional integration. The government has invested heavily in modernizing the transport network, connecting rural areas to urban centers, and improving regional trade corridors.

Key Statistics

  • Total Road Network: 177,800 km
  • Paved Roads: 14,500 km
  • Annual Investment: Ksh 120 billion
  • Jobs Created: 50,000+ direct jobs

Major Projects

Highway

Nairobi Expressway

27.1km elevated toll highway from Mlolongo to Westlands, reducing commute time from 2 hours to 20 minutes during peak hours.

Completed
Highway

Nairobi-Mombasa Highway

Dualing of the 483km highway to improve safety and reduce travel time between Kenya's two largest cities.

Ongoing
Railway

Standard Gauge Railway

Modern railway connecting Mombasa to Nairobi and Naivasha, with plans to extend to Kisumu and Uganda border.

Phase 1 & 2 Completed
Highway

Lamu Port-South Sudan-Ethiopia Transport Corridor (LAPSSET)

Major infrastructure project connecting Kenya to South Sudan and Ethiopia through roads, railway, and port facilities.

Ongoing
Bridge

Likoni Floating Bridge

Modern floating bridge at Likoni channel in Mombasa, easing congestion and improving cross-channel transport.

Completed
Highway

Kenol-Sagana-Marua Dual Carriageway

84km dual carriageway improving access to Mt. Kenya region and reducing accidents on this busy route.

Ongoing
Railway

Nairobi Commuter Rail

Modernization of commuter rail services in Nairobi metropolitan area to reduce traffic congestion.

Ongoing
Highway

Northern Corridor

Upgrading the critical trade route from Mombasa through Nairobi to Uganda, Rwanda, and beyond.

Ongoing

Impact & Benefits

  • Reduced travel time between major cities by up to 40%
  • Improved road safety with modern highway standards
  • Enhanced regional trade and economic integration
  • Created thousands of jobs in construction and transport sectors
  • Opened up rural areas to markets and services
  • Reduced vehicle operating costs and maintenance expenses

Future Plans

The government plans to continue expanding the road network with focus on:

  • Completing ongoing highway dualing projects
  • Expanding the SGR network to cover more regions
  • Modernizing urban public transport systems
  • Improving rural road connectivity
  • Developing smart transport systems with digital solutions
