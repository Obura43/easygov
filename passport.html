Passport Application Guide

Complete guide to applying for a Kenyan passport

Overview

Apply for a Kenyan passport online through the eCitizen portal. The process is straightforward and can be completed from anywhere.

Processing Time & Fees

  • Ordinary Passport (34 pages): Ksh 4,550 - 7-10 working days
  • Ordinary Passport (50 pages): Ksh 6,050 - 7-10 working days
  • Ordinary Passport (66 pages): Ksh 7,550 - 7-10 working days
  • Express Service: Additional Ksh 2,500 - 2 working days

Step-by-Step Application Process

  1. Register on eCitizen

    Visit ecitizen.go.ke and create an account using your ID number and phone number.

  2. Fill Application Form

    Login and select Immigration Services, then fill the passport application form with accurate information.

  3. Upload Documents

    Upload scanned copies of your ID, birth certificate, and a recent passport photo.

  4. Make Payment

    Pay the application fee via M-Pesa, card, or bank transfer.

  5. Book Appointment

    Schedule an appointment at your preferred Huduma Centre for biometric capture.

  6. Visit Huduma Centre

    Attend your appointment with original documents for verification and biometric capture.

  7. Track Application

    Monitor your application status on eCitizen portal.

  8. Collect Passport

    Receive SMS notification and collect your passport from the designated center.

Required Documents

  • Original National ID card
  • Birth certificate (original)
  • Recent passport-size photo (white background)
  • Previous passport (for renewal)
  • Parents' IDs (for first-time applicants under 18)

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a passport?
Standard processing takes 7-10 working days. Express service takes 2 working days with additional fee.
Can I apply for a passport without an ID?
No, a valid National ID is mandatory for passport application. Apply for your ID first through the same eCitizen portal.
What if I lost my previous passport?
Report the loss at the nearest police station, obtain a police abstract, and indicate loss during application.
