County Budgets 2024/2025

Budget allocations across Kenya's 47 counties to support devolved governance

Overview of County Budgets

Kenya’s counties receive an equitable share of the national revenue to fund devolved services in areas such as healthcare, agriculture, infrastructure, and trade development. The allocation formula considers factors like population size, poverty levels, land area, and fiscal responsibility to ensure fairness and promote balanced regional development.

Total County Allocation 2024/2025

Ksh 400.1 billion is distributed among the 47 counties to support service delivery and local development projects. This funding is critical for counties to meet the needs of their residents and implement development plans aligned with the Kenya Vision 2030 and the Big Four Agenda.

Key Priorities in County Budgets

Counties focus their budgets on sectors that directly impact citizen welfare and regional growth:

  • Healthcare and Hospitals: Expanding access to quality medical services and facilities at the county level
  • Road Construction and Maintenance: Developing rural and urban roads to improve connectivity and economic activities
  • Water and Sanitation: Providing clean water and improving sanitation infrastructure to enhance public health
  • Early Childhood Education: Supporting ECD centers and programs for foundational learning
  • Agriculture and Irrigation: Promoting food security through modern farming techniques and irrigation schemes
  • Trade and Market Development: Facilitating local trade through market infrastructure and business support
  • Youth and Women Empowerment: Implementing programs aimed at economic inclusion and skills training

Additional Information

Each county develops its own budget framework reflecting local priorities, with oversight from the County Assemblies to ensure transparency and accountability. Citizens are encouraged to participate in the budgeting process through public forums and consultations.
