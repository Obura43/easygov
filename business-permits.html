Business Permits Services

Your complete guide to obtaining business permits and licenses in Kenya.

Overview

Business permits and licenses are essential for legally operating a business in Kenya. Access these services through government portals such as eCitizen and local county offices.

How to Apply

  1. Step 1:

    Register or login to the relevant government portal (e.g., eCitizen).

  2. Step 2:

    Complete the business permit application form with accurate details.

  3. Step 3:

    Upload the required supporting documents.

  4. Step 4:

    Make the necessary payment for the permit fees.

  5. Step 5:

    Track your application status and collect your business permit once approved.

Required Documents

  • Valid National ID card
  • KRA PIN certificate
  • Business registration certificate
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Any additional documents as requested by the issuing authority
