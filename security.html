Security Projects

Strengthening Kenya's safety and security for sustainable development

Security Projects Overview

The Government of Kenya is committed to enhancing national security through various strategic projects aimed at modernizing security infrastructure, improving law enforcement capacity, and fostering community engagement in security.

Key Security Statistics

  • Police Modernization: Over 5,000 new patrol vehicles deployed nationwide
  • Security Infrastructure: 350 new police posts and stations built or renovated across 40 counties
  • Personnel Training: 10,000 security personnel trained annually in counter-terrorism and cyber security
  • Technology Integration: Installation of 1,500 CCTV cameras in major urban centers
  • Community Policing: Over 20,000 community policing volunteers engaged nationwide

Major Projects

National Police Service Modernization

A comprehensive program aimed at equipping police forces with modern vehicles, communication tools, and forensic equipment to improve response times and investigations.

Status: Ongoing

County Security Posts Expansion

Construction and renovation of police posts in underserved and high-risk counties including Turkana, Mandera, Garissa, and Kilifi to enhance presence and rapid response capabilities.

Status: Ongoing

Cyber Security Enhancement Program

Establishment of a national cyber security center and training of law enforcement in digital crimes, protecting critical infrastructure and citizens from cyber threats.

Status: Launched 2023

Urban CCTV Surveillance Networks

Installation of integrated CCTV surveillance systems in Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, and Eldoret, with real-time monitoring to deter and investigate crime.

Status: Ongoing

Impact

  • Enhanced safety and reduced crime rates in key urban and rural areas
  • Faster emergency response times, improving public confidence
  • Increased community participation in security initiatives
  • Better protection against emerging threats such as cybercrime and terrorism
  • Creation of over 15,000 jobs in security services and technology sectors
