ICT Projects

Transforming Kenya’s digital landscape through innovative technology initiatives

ICT Projects Overview

The Kenyan government has invested over KSh 50 billion in the past five years to expand digital infrastructure, improve internet access, and implement e-government services nationwide. These efforts are part of Vision 2030 to position Kenya as a regional technology hub.

Key ICT Projects

  • National Optic Fibre Backbone Infrastructure (NOFBI): Over 20,000 km of fiber optic cables deployed connecting all 47 counties, increasing internet penetration to 85%.
  • Digital Literacy Programme: Providing digital devices and training to over 1.5 million primary school students to enhance digital skills nationwide.
  • e-Government Services Portal: Centralized digital platform offering over 100 government services online, reducing processing times by up to 60%.
  • Kenya Data Center: Operational since 2022, hosting government data securely, supporting faster service delivery and data privacy.
  • 5G Network Rollout: Pilot projects underway in Nairobi, Mombasa, and Kisumu, aimed at boosting high-speed internet and innovation.

Impact

  • Enhanced internet connectivity benefiting over 45 million Kenyans
  • Creation of over 15,000 jobs in ICT infrastructure, services, and innovation sectors
  • Improved access to government services reducing physical visits and queues
  • Boosted e-commerce and digital payments fueling economic growth
  • Increased digital literacy among youth and underserved communities
