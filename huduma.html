Huduma Services

Your complete guide to accessing Huduma Kenya services across government platforms.

Overview

Huduma Kenya provides integrated government services to citizens via Huduma Centres, online portals, and mobile platforms to simplify access to key public services efficiently.

How to Apply

  1. Step 1:

    Register or log in to the Huduma Kenya online portal or visit a Huduma Centre near you.

  2. Step 2:

    Fill in the required application forms based on the specific service you need.

  3. Step 3:

    Upload or submit required documents as instructed.

  4. Step 4:

    Make any required payments online or at Huduma Centres.

  5. Step 5:

    Track your application status online or via Huduma Centre counters and collect your documents when ready.

Required Documents

  • Valid National ID card
  • KRA PIN certificate (where applicable)
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Other supporting documents depending on the service

Popular Huduma Services

  • National ID application and replacement
  • Birth and death certificates
  • Business registration
  • Driving license applications
  • Passport applications
  • Police clearance certificates
  • Land search and title deed applications
