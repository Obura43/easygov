NTSA Services

Your complete guide to NTSA TIMS services and applications in Kenya.

Overview

The National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) manages driving licenses, vehicle registration, inspection, PSV licensing, and road safety regulations. All NTSA services are accessed online using a TIMS account.

👉 Access NTSA TIMS Portal Here

NTSA Services Available on TIMS

  • Smart Driving License application & renewal
  • New Driving License endorsement
  • Vehicle registration (new & used)
  • Transfer of vehicle ownership
  • Vehicle inspection booking
  • PSV badge application
  • Duplicate logbook application
  • Number plate replacement
  • Motor dealer services

How to Access NTSA Services

  1. Step 1: Create or Log in to a TIMS Account

    Visit the NTSA TIMS portal and register using your ID, phone number, and KRA PIN.

  2. Step 2: Select the Service Category

    Choose the service you need from options such as Smart DL, vehicle registration, inspection, or transfers.

  3. Step 3: Fill in Required Details

    Enter accurate personal, vehicle, or service details as requested on the portal.

  4. Step 4: Upload Required Documents

    Upload scanned copies of ID, KRA PIN, photos, and any other required documents.

  5. Step 5: Make Payment

    Pay using mobile money or bank options available in the TIMS system.

  6. Step 6: Track Status & Collect Your Service

    Track progress through your TIMS dashboard. Once processed, collect the Smart DL or vehicle documents from the selected NTSA office.

Required Documents

  • National ID Card
  • KRA PIN Certificate
  • Passport-sized photo
  • Printed appointment slip (where needed)
  • Supporting documents depending on service (logbook, sale agreement, insurance, etc.)
