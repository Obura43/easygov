Education Projects

National education development initiatives enhancing access, quality, and infrastructure.

Education Projects Overview

The Kenyan government continues to invest heavily in education, committing over KSh 20 billion in recent years to improve learning outcomes, infrastructure, and accessibility. These initiatives focus on expanding educational opportunities for over 8 million students nationwide.

Key Projects and Impact

  • School Infrastructure Expansion: Construction and renovation of 1,500 classrooms and 250 modern laboratories in counties including Nairobi, Kisumu, Nakuru, and Turkana, benefiting more than 500,000 students.
  • Free and Compulsory Education Program: Enrolment of over 1.2 million additional children in primary and secondary schools by eliminating tuition fees in public institutions.
  • Digital Learning Initiatives: Deployment of e-learning platforms and provision of tablets to over 300,000 students in remote areas such as Mandera, Wajir, and Marsabit to bridge the digital divide.
  • Teacher Training and Capacity Building: Professional development programs benefiting over 50,000 teachers to improve teaching standards and learner outcomes.
  • Scholarship and Bursary Programs: Allocation of more than KSh 2 billion annually to support needy and gifted students across all 47 counties.

Expected Outcomes

  • Increase national literacy rate to over 85% by 2030.
  • Improve transition rate from primary to secondary school to 90%.
  • Expand access to quality education in marginalized and arid regions.
  • Enhance digital literacy for 1 million students by 2027.
