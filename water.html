Water & Irrigation Projects

Ensuring water security and food self-sufficiency for all Kenyans

Overview

Kenya’s water and irrigation projects are designed to improve access to clean and reliable water sources while supporting agricultural productivity through sustainable irrigation systems. With over 60% of the country facing water scarcity challenges, these initiatives are crucial for food security, economic growth, and climate resilience.

Key Statistics

  • Over 20 million people gaining improved water access
  • 1.5 million acres under modern irrigation schemes
  • Multiple multi-purpose dams increasing water storage capacity by 1.2 billion cubic meters
  • Thousands of smallholder farmers benefiting from irrigation support

Major Projects

Dam

Thwake Dam

Multi-purpose dam providing water supply, irrigation, and hydroelectric power to Makueni and neighboring counties.

Impact

  • Increased agricultural productivity and food security
  • Improved access to clean drinking water for millions
  • Enhanced climate resilience through sustainable water management
  • Creation of jobs in construction, farming, and water management
  • Reduction in waterborne diseases due to better water quality
