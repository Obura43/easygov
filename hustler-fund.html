Hustler Fund Services

Your trusted guide to accessing Hustler Fund loans and digital credit services in Kenya.

Overview

The Hustler Fund is a government-backed financial inclusion program offering affordable credit to individuals, groups, and small businesses in Kenya. The service is accessible digitally through mobile networks and government platforms.

Main Hustler Fund Services

  • Personal Loans (Individual Micro Loans)
  • Group & Chama Loans
  • SME Loans
  • Loan Limit Growth
  • Loan Repayment & Statements
  • Digital Savings & Interest Earnings

How to Apply for Hustler Fund

  1. Step 1:

    Dial *254# or access via the official Hustler Fund mobile app.

  2. Step 2:

    Select the loan product you want to apply for.

  3. Step 3:

    Enter the requested details such as ID, phone number, and KRA PIN.

  4. Step 4:

    Review loan terms and accept to confirm.

  5. Step 5:

    Receive loan disbursement instantly to your mobile wallet.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Valid Kenyan National ID
  • Registered and active mobile number (Safaricom, Airtel, or Telkom)
  • KRA PIN certificate
  • 18 years and above
  • Active mobile money wallet

Repayment Information

Hustler Fund loans must be repaid within the stipulated repayment period to avoid penalties and to increase your future loan limit. Repayment can be done through the same USSD code or mobile app.
