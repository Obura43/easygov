NSSF Services

Your complete guide to accessing all NSSF services in Kenya.

Overview

The National Social Security Fund (NSSF) provides retirement benefits to Kenyan workers. Services such as registration, contributions, statements, and benefits claims are now accessible online through digital government portals.

Main NSSF Services

  • NSSF Member Registration
  • Employer Registration
  • Employee Contribution Management
  • NSSF Statement Download
  • Benefit Claims & Processing
  • Card Replacement Services

How to Apply

  1. Step 1:

    Visit the official NSSF portal or eCitizen digital platform.

  2. Step 2:

    Create an account or log in using your ID and phone number.

  3. Step 3:

    Fill in the required application forms accurately.

  4. Step 4:

    Upload supporting documents where needed.

  5. Step 5:

    Submit your application and wait for confirmation.

Required Documents

  • National ID (Mandatory)
  • KRA PIN Certificate
  • Passport-size Photo
  • Employment Letter (for some services)
  • Old Membership Number (if available)
