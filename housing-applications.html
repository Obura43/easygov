Housing Applications Services

Your complete guide to accessing government housing application services in Kenya.

Overview

Access government housing application services through official digital platforms, including affordable housing schemes and public housing projects.

How to Apply

  1. Step 1:

    Register or login to the official housing application portal.

  2. Step 2:

    Complete the housing application form with accurate details.

  3. Step 3:

    Upload required supporting documents.

  4. Step 4:

    Make any necessary payments or deposits as required.

  5. Step 5:

    Track your application status online and attend interviews or inspections if needed.

Required Documents

  • Valid National ID card
  • KRA PIN certificate
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Proof of income or employment (if required)
  • Any other documents specified in the application guidelines

Popular Housing Programs

  • Affordable Housing Program
  • Kenya Mortgage Refinance Company (KMRC) Schemes
  • Youth Housing Fund
  • Women Housing Fund
  • County Housing Projects
