Housing Projects

Transforming Kenya's housing landscape through strategic national initiatives

Housing Projects Overview

The Kenyan government has committed over KSh 120 billion in the last five years to address the national housing deficit, estimated at over 2 million units. These investments focus on affordable housing, urban renewal, and slum upgrading across major counties including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, and Nakuru.

Key Housing Projects

  • Affordable Housing Programme: Delivered over 40,000 affordable units in phases, including developments in Nairobi's Ruai, Athi River, and Kiambu counties.
  • Slum Upgrading: Initiatives in Kibera, Mathare, and Mukuru slums involving infrastructure improvement and provision of serviced plots to 100,000 residents.
  • Urban Renewal: Redevelopment of aging estates like Nairobi's Gikambura and Nairobi CBD to modern mixed-use housing.
  • Mortgage Finance Access: Supported by the Kenya Mortgage Refinancing Company (KMRC), enabling over 15,000 families to access affordable home loans.
  • Green Housing Initiatives: Incorporation of sustainable construction methods and energy-efficient designs in new housing projects.

Impact

  • Increased homeownership for over 100,000 Kenyan families
  • Reduction in informal settlements through slum upgrading programs
  • Job creation for over 20,000 people in construction and allied industries
  • Improved urban infrastructure and living standards in targeted counties
  • Boosted economic growth through housing sector investments contributing over 5% to GDP
